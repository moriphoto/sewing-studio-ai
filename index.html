b59 Embroidery Setup

From unbox to ready to stitch — follows the official manual visuals.

  1. Switch on & select Embroidery mode on the Home Screen.
  2. Attach the Embroidery Module (slides in from left to right until it clicks).
  3. Fit the Hopper Embroidery Foot and embroidery needle.
  4. Prepare the hoop with the template and hoop the fabric taut.
  5. Attach the hoop to the module until it clicks and is recognised.
  6. Thread bobbin and upper embroidery thread.
  7. Load design and press start.
